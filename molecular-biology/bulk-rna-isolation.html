Bulk RNA Isolation — human brain tissue

TRIzol / Direct-zol (Zymo) workflow with baked Dounce homogenizer. Target RIN > 7 for most archived tissue; 8+ for fresh surgical.
Preparation matters more than extraction: most low-RIN failures trace to a warm Dounce or RNase contamination, not the column cleanup. Bake Dounces (Route A) between tissue categories; use the fast RNaseZap-only wash (Route B) only within a same-day same-category batch.

Preparation — Dounce cleaning protocol

Separate vessel and pestle; keep matched pairs together. Full detergent + bake protocol (Route A) ensures RNase destruction; fast between-sample cleaning (Route B) is same-day same-category only.

Route A — Full clean (between tissue categories)

0 of 0 checked

Step 1 — Detergent wash (removes organic residue)

#Action
1aRinse vessel and pestle under warm tap water to remove loose debris.
1bSubmerge in 1–2% Alconox in warm DI water. Scrub interior of vessel with dedicated test tube brush; scrub pestle shaft + grinding surface with lint-free wipe or soft brush.
1cSoak 15–30 min for old/idle Dounces to loosen any residue.
1dRinse thoroughly with copious DI water — at least 10 volumes. Any detergent residue inhibits downstream enzymes.
1eFinal rinse with nuclease-free water.

Step 2 — Dry

#Action
2aRinse with 100% ethanol to displace water and speed drying.
2bAir-dry inverted on a clean surface, or drying oven at 60°C for 30 min.
2cFully dry before baking — water pockets can cause thermal stress cracks.

Step 3 — Bake (destroys RNases)

#Action
3aWrap vessel and pestle separately in heavy-duty aluminum foil. Fold edges tightly to keep sterile post-bake.
3bPlace in dry-heat oven at 240°C for 4 hours (or 250°C for 2 hours).
3cCool to room temperature inside the oven with foil intact. Do not unwrap until ready to use.
3dIf no bake-capable oven available: proceed to Step 4 (RNaseZap) as chemical-only alternative. Second-best but workable.

Step 4 — RNaseZap treatment (if not baking, or belt-and-suspenders)

#Action
4aFill vessel with RNaseZap. Submerge pestle fully. Leave 10 min.
4bRinse 3× with copious DEPC-treated or nuclease-free water. RNaseZap residue interferes with downstream.
4cRinse with 100% ethanol; air-dry inverted on foil in a covered container.

Step 5 — Chill and use

#Action
5aPre-chill assembled Dounce on ice for at least 10 min before RNA work.
5bHandle by the top only. If touched with bare skin, redo Step 4.

Route B — Fast Between-Sample Cleaning (same-day batch, same category only)

#Action
B1Rinse vessel and pestle with DI water to remove TRIzol and tissue debris.
B2Fill vessel with RNaseZap, submerge pestle, agitate for 30 sec.
B3Rinse 3× with DEPC-treated or nuclease-free water.
B4Rinse with 100% ethanol.
B5Air-dry briefly (or wipe pestle shaft with lint-free wipe), re-chill on ice, proceed.

Do NOT use Route B between different tissue categories (e.g., tumor → normal cortex, or patient A → patient B for downstream comparison). Either dedicate a Dounce per specimen or run Route A.

Bulk RNA isolation protocol

Step 1 — Sample preparation

#Action
1aStart with 20–50 mg tissue. Larger pieces overwhelm the extraction and drop yield/quality.
1bKeep sample frozen until lysis. Every thaw-refreeze cycle costs RIN.
1cRinse in cold PBS before homogenization.
1dChill both pestles on ice.

Step 2 — Dounce Homogenization

#Action
2aPlace tissue into 2 mL Dounce with 1 mL cold TRIzol (or QIAzol). Keep Dounce on ice throughout.
2bLoose pestle (A): 10–15 slow full strokes. Rotate pestle slightly on each stroke. Do not pump.
2cTight pestle (B): 10–15 strokes. Resistance drops as tissue disperses.
2dFor dense GBM stroma or gliotic scar: 20+ tight strokes. Suspension should look uniformly cloudy with no visible fragments.
2eTransfer to 1.5 mL tube. If visible chunks remain: pass through 21G needle 3–4×, or spin 2,000 × g for 2 min and take supernatant.

Step 3 — Phase Separation

#Action
3aIncubate 5 min at room temperature.
3bAdd 200 μL chloroform. Shake vigorously by hand for 15 sec (do not vortex).
3cIncubate 3 min at room temperature.
3dCentrifuge 12,000 × g, 15 min, 4°C.
3eTransfer only the top ~60% of the clear aqueous phase to a new tube. Stay well above the interphase.

Step 4 — Column Cleanup (Direct-zol)

#Action
4aAdd an equal volume of 100% ethanol to the aqueous phase. Mix by pipetting.
4bLoad onto Direct-zol column (in collection tube). Spin per kit (typically 30 sec, 12,000 × g). Discard flow-through.
4cOn-column DNase I treatment (per Zymo): 5 μL DNase I + 75 μL DNA Digestion Buffer. Apply to column, incubate 15 min RT.
4dWash: Direct-zol Pre-Wash (2× per kit), then RNA Wash Buffer.
4eDry column: spin empty at max speed for 1–2 min to remove residual ethanol. Critical for downstream apps.
4fElute in 30–50 μL nuclease-free water. For max yield, apply eluate back to column and spin once more.

Step 5 — QC

#Action
5aMeasure A260/A280 (target ≥1.9) and A260/A230 (target ≥1.8) on NanoDrop.
5bBioanalyzer or TapeStation for RIN before any library prep.
5cAliquot before freezing at −80°C. Avoid freeze-thaw cycles.

Troubleshooting — Dounce cleaning

  • Cloudy or filmy residue after drying: Detergent not fully rinsed. Redo Step 1 with more DI water volumes.
  • Glass fogging or micro-cracks after baking: Residual water at bake start, or oven cooled too fast. Let cool inside oven next time.
  • Low RIN despite clean protocol: Check that Dounce was cold at start of homogenization and that stroke pace didn't warm it. Cleaning is one variable; heat is the more common culprit.
  • Pestle sticks in vessel: Match check — swapped pestle. Or grinding surfaces damaged. Retire.
  • Old grease/oil on ground-glass surfaces of vintage Dounces: Extended acetone soak (10 min in fume hood) before detergent wash. Then full Route A.
  • No dry-heat oven access: Route A Steps 1–2 then Step 4 (RNaseZap only) is acceptable. Skip Step 3. Won't get the same guarantee but works for most bulk RNA applications.

Troubleshooting — Extraction

  • Low RIN unexpectedly: Dounce warming up during work. Re-chill and reduce stroke pace. Check that TRIzol was cold at start.
  • Lipid layer that won't disperse: More tight-pestle strokes. Common with white matter.
  • Low A260/A230: Residual guanidine or ethanol. Extend the dry spin; re-elute.
  • Low yield: Tissue input too small, or too much aqueous phase left behind. Do not overcorrect by taking phase closer to interphase.
  • gDNA contamination on qPCR: On-column DNase step skipped or too short. Repeat with fresh DNase and full 15-min incubation.
  • Cross-contamination between samples: Dedicate Dounces per specimen or wash rigorously (RNaseZap → DEPC water → ice).

Personal reference protocol. Verify oven temperature calibration before relying on dry-heat sterilization. Verify DNase incubation times and column steps against current Zymo Direct-zol kit insert.
