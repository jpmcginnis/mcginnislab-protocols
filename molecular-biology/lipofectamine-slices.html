Lipofectamine + pHelper — human organotypic slices

Add pHelper to temporal lobe slice already carrying library, using Lipofectamine 3000 + P3000. Yields 4 μL of transfection mix per well.

Procedure (per well, 4 μL total)

4Incubate at room temp in hood15 min
5Add the 4 μL to library slice4 μL
