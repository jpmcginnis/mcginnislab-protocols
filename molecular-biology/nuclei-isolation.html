Nuclei Isolation for Single-Nucleus RNAseq — old way, from Suyang Bao (Arenkiel Lab)

Adapted from Yanyan Qi's (Hongjie Li Lab) "single-nucleus RNAseq" protocol and Isolation and RNA sequencing of single nuclei from Drosophila tissues (DOI: 10.1016/j.xpro.2022.101417). Buffer recipe adapted from 10x Genomics demonstrated protocol Nuclei Isolation from Cell Suspensions & Tissues for Single Cell RNA Sequencing, CG000124 Rev F. Protocol version 1.5, Sep 22 2023.
Critical:
  • All steps on ice or 4°C.
  • Buffers made fresh the day of extraction, on ice, stored on ice.
  • Avoid vortexing — may damage nuclei. Pipette to mix instead.

Preparations

  1. Clean Dounce homogenizer set (≥1 day in advance): wash with tap water, then 70% ethanol (1 mL into glass tube, pestle A >5 strokes, pestle B >5 strokes, discard ethanol). Rinse with ddH₂O under running MilliQ, then with 1 mL nuclease-free water (pestle A then B, aspirate). Autoclave in sterilization pouches. (Lab: JP bench, top shelf.)
  2. Clean bench top & pipettes with 70% ethanol, then RNaseZAP.
  3. Buffer preparation: make fresh on the day of extraction.

Procedure

0 of 0 checked

Nuclei isolation

#Action
1If using frozen sample, thaw from −80°C on ice completely (OB: 5–8 min).
2Add 1 mL lysis buffer. Carefully transfer sample + lysis buffer into Dounce glass tube.
3Release nuclei: ~20 strokes loose pestle A (no small chunks visible), then 20–40 strokes tight pestle B (OB: 30 strokes). Keep Dounce on ice; avoid bubbles.
4Filter 1 mL through 35 μm cell strainer cap into 5 mL FACS tube.
5(Optional, recommended) Coat EP tubes: add 300 μL 1% BSA, vortex, discard.
6Filter samples through 40 μm Flowmi cell strainer into 1.5 mL EP tube. (JP bench, top shelf.)
7Centrifuge 5 min × 500 × g at 4°C. Max: 10 min × 1000 × g. Lab variant: we used 900 × g for 10 min.
8Discard supernatant, keep nuclei pellet intact.
9Resuspend nuclei pellet in 300–600 μL resuspension buffer (OB: 500 μL). Pipette ~30× to completely resuspend.
10Filter samples again through 40 μm Flowmi cell strainer into new 1.5 mL EP tube. (Can be skipped for OB; JP skips this.)
11Filter samples through 35 μm cell strainer cap into new 5 mL FACS tube. (RNA cabinet.)

FACS sorting (Sony MA900, 100 μm chip)

#Action
1Prepare unstained control: 20 μL unstained nuclei into 200–250 μL resuspension buffer in new FACS tube. Used for FACS gate setup.
2Stain samples in FACS tube with Hoechst-33342 (1:500). Pipette briefly to mix. Incubate >5 min.
3Set up sorter prior to nuclear extraction (30–60 min). Pre-cool sample + collection chambers to 4°C.
4Load unstained control first to set negative gate.
5Coat each collection tube: 1 mL 1% BSA in 5 mL FACS tube, vortex, discard.
6Load collection tube with 10× Collection Buffer sized to estimated final collection volume (e.g. 4 mL final → 400 μL 10× buffer).
7Load Hoechst-stained nuclei + collection tube. Gate tightly on Hoechst-high population to exclude debris. Use FSC/SSC to separate singlets from multiplets.
8Sort 100–200k nuclei per 10x run (minimum ~100k).
9Evaluate quality + concentration on C-Chip under microscope. Centrifuge 10 min × 1000 × g at 4°C (swinging bucket). Discard supernatant without touching pellet; resuspend in lower volume. Re-count on C-Chip, adjust volume. (Typically ~16k nuclei loaded per 10x run.) Temporarily save supernatant on ice until resuspended nuclei verified.

Lab notes

  • 12/4/23 batch: sampled 1/23/24. Had abnormal-shaped nuclei esp. in day14+BC comparison, pre-sorted d14 untreated to sorted d14 untreated — presort nuclei much more normal. snRNA-seq quality ended up just fine.
  • Scale notes: for 8/21 samples used 1 mL lysis buffer per sample (−20°C, 16 slices discs); for 12/4/23 used 4 tubes (~32 discs) per sample, which gave really big nuclei pellet — could have used ½ or ¼ even starting material.
  • If >4 samples, two people work in parallel to shorten total time.
