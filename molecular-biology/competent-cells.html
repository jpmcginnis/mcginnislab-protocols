Chemical Competent Cell Production (rubidium chloride)

Lab-standard RbCl method based on Promega Protocols and Applications Guide (3rd edition, p. 45–46). Yields ~1.5 × 10⁷ cfu/μg; cells usable for up to a year at −70°C.
Lab-typical scale: 500 mL growth culture (2 × 250 mL in centrifuge bottles). Uses 100 mL TFB1 and 20 mL TFB2 total. Keep everything ice-cold from Step 3 onward.

Procedure

0 of 0 checked
#Action
1Inoculate a single colony from an LB plate (or a scoop from glycerol stock) into 20 mL SOC media in 125 mL flask. Incubate overnight at 37°C with shaking (approximately 225 rpm). Keep everything sterile since you do not use any antibiotics.
2On the following day, use 5 mL of the overnight culture to inoculate 500 mL of LB medium containing 20 mM MgSO₄ (2.46 g MgSO₄ in 500 mL LB broth). This results in a 1:100 dilution. Grow the cells in a 1 L baffled flask until the A₆₀₀ reaches 0.4–0.6 (typically 2–3 h). A 1 L flask is necessary for proper aeration.
3Pellet the cells by centrifugation at 4,500 × g for 5 min at 4°C. For a 500 mL culture, use two 250 mL centrifuge bottles in a large rotor (Sorvall GSA, Beckman JA-14). Quick-rinse each pellet with a little TFB1 to remove all traces of media.
4Gently resuspend the cell pellets in 0.4 × original culture volume of ice-cold TFB1 — 100 mL TFB1 total, 50 mL per bottle — using a 10 mL serological pipette. Combine the resuspended cells in one bottle. For the remaining steps, keep cells on ice and chill all pipets, tubes, and flasks.
5Incubate the resuspended cells on ice for 5 minutes.
6Pellet the cells by centrifugation at 4,500 × g for 5 min at 4°C.
7Gently resuspend the cells in 1/25 of the original culture volume of ice-cold TFB2 — 20 mL TFB2 for us.
Note: Treat the competent cells gently; they are highly sensitive to handling and elevated temperature.
8Incubate the cells on ice for 15–60 minutes, then aliquot into prechilled tubes. Quick-freeze the tubes in liquid N₂ and store at −70°C.
9Test competency by transforming 100 pg of a plasmid. Heat shock for 45 sec at 42°C.

Additional notes

  1. Cells do NOT resuspend easily in TFB1. Be patient and gentle (pipet up and down, swirl the bottle — do not vortex). A perfectly uniform suspension is uncommon; small clumps are OK. Keep bottles on ice while resuspending in TFB1.
  2. Cells resuspend much more easily in TFB2. Usually let sit 45 min to 1 h before aliquoting.
  3. Expected competency with this procedure: ~1.5 × 10⁷ cfu/μg. Cells usable for up to a year at −70°C.
  4. Heat shock for 45 sec at 42°C during test transformation.
