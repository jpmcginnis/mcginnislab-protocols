Papain Dissociation

Dissociate organotypic slices into single cells for downstream plating / transduction. Adapted from Park et al., Nature Protocols 2022.

Procedure

0 of 0 checked
#Action
1Place slices approx. size of 4–6 discs in warmed 400 μL papain + DNase.
2Incubate + shaking at 37°C for 5 min.
3Triturate slowly in p1000 ~5×.
4Incubate for 5 min.
5Triturate again.
6Incubate 15 min.
7Spin at 170 × g for 7 min; remove supernatant.
8Resuspend in OSCM; aliquot 100 μL into wells.

Lab notes

  • 11/3: estimated 84,200 cells per 100 μL (plated 100 μL per well) by Countess. Transduced at 6000 MOI.

Source

Adapted from Park TI, Smyth LCD, Aalderink M, Woolf ZR, et al. Routine culture and study of adult human brain cells from neurosurgical specimens. Nature Protocols 17, 190–221 (2022). doi:10.1038/s41596-021-00637-8
