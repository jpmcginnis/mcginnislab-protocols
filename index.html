Media Formulations

  • OSCM — Organotypic Slice Culture Medium (1 L)
    Defined serum-free medium for ex vivo organotypic human brain slice culture. 40 components, good for 2 weeks at 4°C.
  • Miles Media (100 mL)
    Defined serum-free medium with tighter composition control than OSCM. 9-step batch protocol, 7 combined + 9 individual + bulk protein stocks + 6 fresh-weighed reagents. Make fresh weekly; 7-day shelf life at 4°C.
  • Adam's Media (100 mL)
    Neurobasal-based medium with weighed-in bulk salts for HEPES buffering (Neurobasal is HEPES-free). Simplest and most stable of the comparison set; 14-day shelf life.
  • Ting's Media (20 mL)
    MEM Eagle + horse serum + insulin + fresh ascorbic acid. Shortest shelf life (3 days), matches feeding schedule. Lower incubator temp (35°C) and humidified.
  • BrainPhys (100 mL)
    All-commercial STEMCELL formulation. Three liquids combined; no growth factors (by design, to avoid confounds in the media comparison). ~5–10 min total.
