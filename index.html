Artificial CSF

  • Artificial CSF (NMDG-SCS) — 500 mL
    NMDG-based slice cutting / perfusion solution. 11 powders weighed, pH to 7.3–7.4 with 5 M HCl, osm titrated to 295–305.

Media Formulations

  • OSCM — Organotypic Slice Culture Medium (1 L)
    Defined serum-free medium for ex vivo organotypic brain slice culture. 40 components; 2-week shelf life at 4°C.
  • Miles Media (100 mL)
    Defined serum-free, tighter composition than OSCM. 9-step batch, combined + individual + bulk protein stocks + fresh-weighed reagents. 7-day shelf life.
  • Adam's Media (100 mL)
    Neurobasal + weighed-in HEPES/salts (Neurobasal is HEPES-free). Simplest of the comparison set; 14-day shelf.
  • Ting's Media (20 mL)
    MEM Eagle + horse serum + insulin + fresh ascorbic acid. 3-day shelf; 35°C humidified incubator.
  • BrainPhys (100 mL)
    All-commercial STEMCELL formulation. Three liquids; no growth factors (comparison design). ~5–10 min.

Molecular Biology

  • Miniprep plasmid isolation (centrifuge)
    Invitrogen PureLink Quick Plasmid Miniprep. 9 steps: harvest, resuspend, lyse, precipitate, bind, wash (opt), wash+remove ethanol, elute, recover.
  • Chemical competent cells (RbCl)
    Rubidium chloride competent-cell prep per Promega. 500 mL growth, TFB1/TFB2 buffers, aliquot & LN₂ freeze. ~1.5 × 10⁷ cfu/μg.
  • Annealing oligos
    PCR-tube anneal of complementary oligos from 100 μM stocks in CutSmart. 95°C → 25°C ramp over 45 min. Dilute 100× after.
  • Nuclei isolation (old way — Suyang Bao)
    snRNA-seq nuclei prep from flash-frozen tissue. Dounce homogenize, filter, Hoechst stain, FACS sort on Sony MA900. Adapted from Yanyan Qi / 10x Genomics CG000124.
  • Papain dissociation
    Dissociate 4–6 slice discs in warmed papain+DNase, triturate, spin, resuspend in OSCM. ~84k cells per 100 μL by Countess.
  • Lipofectamine with human slices
    Add pHelper to library-carrying slice via Lipofectamine 3000 + P3000. 4 μL mix per well, 15 min RT incubation.
  • Bulk RNA isolation (human brain)
    TRIzol/Direct-zol workflow with full Dounce cleaning (bake + RNaseZap) as preparation. Expected RIN table by specimen type; cleaning + extraction troubleshooting.

Histology & Imaging

  • Live/Dead staining
    Molecular Probes LIVE/DEAD + Hoechst nuclear counterstain on organotypic slices. Methanol dead control; 4% PFA fix; STED imaging on 9th floor.
