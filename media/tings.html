Ting's Media — 20 mL batch

MEM Eagle + horse serum + insulin + fresh ascorbic acid. Shortest shelf life of the set; matches feeding schedule.
pH7.2–7.3
Shelf life3 days at 4°C
Make freshEvery 2–3 days
Incubator35°C / 5% CO₂, humidified
Ascorbic acid must be dissolved fresh and used immediately. Horse serum: gentle mix only — don't vortex. Note the lower incubator temp (35°C) and higher humidity vs. the other media.

Documented deviations from Ting's paper

Using liquid MEM with Earle's salts + glutamine (Gibco 11095-098) rather than making from powder means several components come from the MEM baseline rather than being added separately:

  • NaHCO₃: ~26 mM (from MEM baseline) vs. paper's 15 mM
  • CaCl₂: 1.8 mM (from MEM baseline) vs. paper's 1 mM
  • L-glutamine: ~2 mM (from MEM) vs. paper's 0.5 mM GlutaMAX
  • No GlutaMAX added — MEM has built-in glutamine

Handling notes

  • Horse serum: heat-inactivate (56°C × 30 min) once when first opened, then aliquot into 4 mL portions at −20°C. Each batch thaws one aliquot.
  • Insulin working stock: 1 mg/mL diluted from vendor stock; kept −20°C.
  • Ascorbic acid: dissolve fresh for each batch; use within minutes.
