OSCM — Organotypic Slice Culture Medium (1 L)

Defined serum-free medium for ex vivo organotypic human brain slice culture. Hover any reagent for vendor, catalog #, storage, and (eventually) location.
pH7.35–7.45
Osmolarity300–320 mOsm
Shelf life2 weeks at 4°C
Add components in the order listed. The 300 mL initial water is a dissolving solvent; the final ~600 mL should be added in smaller portions, titrating to reach osmolarity 300–320 mOsm.

Notes

  • Lab aliquot convention: most −20°C reagents that aren't commercially ready-made are pre-aliquoted at 1000× (add 1 mL per 1 L recipe). A handful of small-volume reagents are pre-aliquoted at 10,000× (add 100 μL per 1 L recipe) — flagged in the Volume column.
  • Ready-made exceptions (not aliquoted): BME, DMEM/F12, H₂O, KCl, NaCl, NaHCO₃, ZnSO₄, Sodium pyruvate, Sodium lactate, BSA, Mannitol, N-2 supplement, Insulin, Glycerol, GlutaMax, Acetic acid.
  • Powder reagents (Creatine, HEPES): weigh directly for 1 L recipe rather than making a stock.
  • Removed from original recipe: Heparin (Sigma H3149) — struck out; not included.
  • Scale: published Nature Protocols recipe is for 100 mL; this page shows the 1 L version.
