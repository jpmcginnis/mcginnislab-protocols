All-commercial STEMCELL formulation. Simplest of the media-comparison set — three liquids combined.

Handling notes

  • SM1 and N2-A arrive frozen. Thaw once, aliquot into 2 mL and 1 mL portions respectively, store −20°C. Don't repeatedly freeze/thaw.
  • BrainPhys is refrigerated only, not frozen.
  • No labile components — 2 weeks at 4°C is standard STEMCELL guidance for the finished medium.
