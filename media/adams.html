Handling notes

  • Neurobasal is HEPES-free — the 15 mM HEPES weighed in is what buffers Adam's.
  • B-27 variant lock: must be Gibco 17504044 (matches other conditions using B-27 in the comparison).
  • Pen-Strep → AB/AM: the McGinnis Lab uses AB/AM (antibiotic/antimycotic) instead of plain Pen-Strep, which also covers fungal contamination.
