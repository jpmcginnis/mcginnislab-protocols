Human CSF Processing — from fresh collection to −80°C aliquots

Processing hCSF for use as a slice-culture additive. Centrifuge → sterile filter → osmolarity check → aliquot → freeze.
Centrifuge2000 g, 4°C, 10 min
Filter0.2 µm
Target osmolarity280 ± 20 mOsm/L
Aliquot5–10 mL
Store−80°C
Process immediately after collection. hCSF should go from the collection container straight to this workflow — don't leave it sitting on the bench.

Procedure

  1. Centrifuge. 2000 g, 4°C, 10 min. This pellets cells and debris.
  2. Sterile filter. Pass the supernatant through a 0.2 µm filter into a sterile receiver.
  3. Check osmolarity. Measure every batch on the osmometer (Osmette 5002 A quick protocol).
    • Target: 280 mOsm/L, deviation range ±20 mOsm/L.
    • Record the value against the batch ID.
  4. Aliquot. 5–10 mL per aliquot in sterile cryovials or tubes. Label with batch ID, patient numbers combined, and date.
  5. Freeze at −80°C until use.
Combine CSF from at least three different patients before using. No slice-culture batch should ever receive single-donor CSF — pool across at least three different patient numbers.

Source

Processing conditions (2000 g / 4°C / 10 min, 0.2 µm filter, 280 ± 20 mOsm/L, 5–10 mL aliquots at −80°C) follow the hCSF slice-culture medium prep protocol as published [ref 30 in the source method].
