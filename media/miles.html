Miles Media — 100 mL batch

Defined serum-free medium for organotypic slice culture. Make fresh weekly; shorter shelf life than OSCM but tighter composition control.
pH7.2–7.4
Shelf life7 days at 4°C
Make freshWeekly
Incubator37°C / 5% CO₂
Finished batch is filtered 0.22 μm PES, then equilibrated in CO₂ incubator ≥30 min before slice plating. Oxaloacetate must be used within 15 min of dissolving.

Prerequisite stocks

Made once, used across many batches. Reference only — not part of per-batch checklist below.

7 combined stocks (100 μL aliquots at −20°C)

  • Stable amino acid mix — water, 30 mL total prep
  • Steroid + fat-soluble vitamin mix — 100% ethanol, 30 mL, amber
  • Stable water-soluble vitamin mix — water, 4.5 mL (myo-inositol limited)
  • Trace metals mix — acidified water pH 3, 30 mL (excludes CuSO₄)
  • Photolabile B-vitamin mix — 0.01 N NaOH, 30 mL, amber
  • Alkaline amino acid mix — 0.1 N NaOH, 30 mL
  • Mg / phosphate / citrate mix — water, 30 mL

Individual 1000× aqueous stocks (100 μL each at −20°C)

  • ATP, Phosphocreatine, SOD, Glycerol, Na-lactate, Na-β-hydroxybutyrate, Putrescine, Thymidine, T3
  • Heparin was listed in Miles's original but struck out in this lab's version — excluded here (9 stocks total instead of 10).

Bulk protein stocks (−20°C)

  • BSA 25% w/v — thaw 1.06 mL per batch
  • Transferrin 5 mg/mL — 100 μL per batch (corrected from Miles's transcription error)

Insulin working stock

  • Insulin — use OSCM stocks

Shared Park OSCM stocks (1000×)

  • Corticosterone — 1000× ethanol
  • α-Tocopherol — 1000× ethanol
  • 17β-Estradiol — 1000× ethanol
  • CuSO₄ — 1000× aqueous

Notes

  • Heparin discrepancy: Miles's original Step 3 said "10 individual small stocks = 1.0 mL" but Heparin was struck out of the stock list in this lab's annotation. Current protocol uses 9 stocks = 900 μL. If the full 10 should be restored, flag it.
  • Transferrin volume: page 1 of the source note says "corrected from Miles's transcription error" — 100 μL per 100 mL batch is the corrected value used here.
  • Oxaloacetate: dissolve in COLD water and use within 15 min. Highly unstable in solution.
  • Insulin: uses OSCM's working stock — don't make a separate aliquot for Miles.
  • Checklist: state saved in your browser only (localStorage), per-device. Refresh-safe. Hit "Reset checklist" when starting a fresh batch.
