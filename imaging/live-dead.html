Live/Dead Staining

Protocol utilizes Live/Dead Protocol from Molecular Probes/Invitrogen. Organotypic tissue from cranial tissue resected from neurosurgical specimens, cultured for a variable number of days, and taken out of culture for staining. The protocol details the staining and fixative procedure for downstream imaging.

Materials

  • organotypic tissue
  • LIVE/DEAD Viability/Cytotoxicity Kit for mammalian cells
    • Reagents should be stored sealed, desiccated, protected from light, and frozen at under −20°C.
    • Allow reagents to warm to room temperature and centrifuge briefly.
    • Prepare Calcein AM in aqueous working solutions immediately prior to use, and use within one day.
  • 1× PBS for cell culture
  • Hoechst
  • 4% PFA in 1× PBS
  • rubber well(s)
  • glass slides
  • cover slips
  • OCT
  • embedding molds
Handwritten per-slice recipe (annotation on page 1): 1 mL PBS · 2 μL EthD-1 · 0.5 μL Calcein AM · 0.5 μL Hoechst.

Procedure

0 of 0 checked

Preparing solutions

#Action
1Remove the LIVE/DEAD Reagent from the freezer and allow them to warm to room temperature.
2Add 20 μL of the supplied 2 mM EthD-1 stock solution to 10 mL of sterile tissue-culture-grade D-PBS. Vortex to ensure thorough mixing. This gives an approximate 4 μM EthD-1 solution.
3Combine reagents by transferring 5 μL of Calcein AM stock solution to 10 mL EthD-1 solution. Vortex. + 0.5 μL Hoechst (per handwritten annotation).
4Dead control: Prepare 70% methanol solution. Be extremely careful — this solution is highly toxic.

LIVE/DEAD Stain

#Action
5Transfer tissue OUT of plate into new plate in cell culture hood.
6Remove this plate with single slice out of the cell culture hood.
7Add 70% methanol in fume hood. DO NOT DO IN CELL CULTURE HOOD.
8Wait 30 minutes for methanol-incubated tissue.
9After 30 minutes, take another slice from the plate, and move to new plate.
10Move plate out of cell culture hood.
11To both plates, remove any liquid and wash 1× PBS 1 time for 5 min.
12Add LIVE/DEAD solution from Step 3. Incubate for 45 minutes. Up to 2 hours.
13Fix both tissue samples for 30 min in 4% PFA.
14Wash 1× with 1× PBS.

Nuclear Staining

#Action
15Add Hoechst nuclear stain (1:1000×) to both tissue samples, and wash 3× with PBS.
16Cut each tissue sample in half.
17Mount one half of each tissue sample immediately on deep well slide with fluoromount and image. Image on STED scope on 9th floor.
18Place other half of each tissue sample in 30% sucrose overnight. Freeze slice with OCT the following day and slice on cryostat at 20–50 μm. Mount on slides; image using STED 9th floor.

Imaging

#Action
19Use these channels:
  • Blue Channel / Nuclear (ex: 350 nm / em: 461 nm)
  • Green Channel / Live Cells (ex: ~495 nm / em: ~515 nm)
  • Red Channel / Dead Cells (ex: ~528 / 617 nm)
20Take z-stack appropriate to slices.

Quantification

#Action
21Use imaris. Ask EKT.
