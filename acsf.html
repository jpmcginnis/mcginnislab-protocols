Artificial CSF — 500 mL (NMDG-SCS)

NMDG-based slice cutting / perfusion solution for ex vivo brain tissue. Always made in 500 mL batches in this lab.
Final volume500 mL
pH7.3–7.4
Osmolarity295–305 mOsm
After weighing all powders and combining:
  • Add 420 mL Thermo/Invitrogen distilled water.
  • Measure pH; adjust down to 7.3–7.4 with 5 M HCl, 100–200 μL at a time.
  • Once pH is set, measure osmolarity; add water to reach 295–305 mOsm.
    • If osm ~340, you can safely add <30 mL water.
    • If osm ~320, you can safely add <10 mL water.

Notes

  • Variant: OSCM-H (High HEPES-supplemented OSCM) — add an extra 1.787 g HEPES (15 mM) on top of the 20 mM already in the recipe above. Not used for standard aCSF.
  • Order of operations: all weigh-ins first, then combine, then water, then pH, then osm. pH adjustment overshooting slightly with NaOH is tolerable but adds steps — go slow with HCl.
  • Water: Thermo/Invitrogen distilled water specifically (same catalog as the cell-culture water used elsewhere).
