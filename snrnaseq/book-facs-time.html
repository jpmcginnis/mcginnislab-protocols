How to book FACS time — BCM Cytometry and Cell Sorting Core

Reserve the Sony MA900 and confirm a technician is available for the slot. Sorter used for our nuclei isolation workflow.

Procedure

0 of 0 checked
#Action
1 Log in to the BCM iLab scheduler: bcm.ilab.agilent.com/account/login.
2 Click the three-horizontal-lines menu (top left), then find and click "Cytometry and Cell Sorting Core".
3 You start on the "Schedule Equipment" tab. Scroll down to "Cell Sorting" and click. Then click the line with the Sony MA900.
4 Scroll down and click through days to find a day where the Sony MA900 is free for several hours beginning around 11am or so. Make note of the date and time.
5 Click and drag at least 3 hours, beginning 30 min before you plan on showing up.
6 Fill in the form's right side as shown below:
  • Match the sorter: Sony MA900 M903
  • What kind of appointment: Assisted
  • Cell type: Human
  • Are the cells fixed? No
  • Chip size and pressure: 130 μm / 9 psi
  • Fluorophores: DAPI
  • Mode of collection: 5 mL tubes
  • What you're sorting: Nuclei sorting
  • Other prompts: "Other"; "Yes — on this instrument and everything is the same"; Yes to previous consultation; Yes to NucBlu DAPI; "I agree"
Submit / reserve.
iLab reservation form — fields filled in as above.
7 Click back to "Cytometry and Cell Sorting Core". Scroll down to the technicians' schedule at the "Staff Availability" tab, click, and select "CCSC Staff Availability".
8 Make sure a technician is available at the time you need. If not, you'll have to find a new time where the MA900 is available AND a technician is available.
9 They will send you confirmations of the time.

Notes

  • Start the reservation 30 min earlier than you plan to arrive — you need buffer for setup.
  • Lab default: Sony MA900 (M903), 130 μm chip, 9 psi, DAPI, 5 mL collection tubes. Human nuclei, unfixed.
  • The core sends a confirmation email after both the equipment reservation and the technician availability are confirmed.
  • Pair this with the Nuclei isolation (Miltenyi gentleMACS, FACS core) protocol — the sort happens immediately after the 30 μm filter step.
