Nuclei Isolation — Miltenyi Nuclei Extraction Buffer + gentleMACS, at FACS core

First step of the snRNA-seq pipeline. Based on Miltenyi Biotec Nuclei Extraction Buffer manual (order no. 130-128-024). Lab adaptations in yellow throughout.
DRAFT — this page is a first-pass transcription of the Miltenyi manual with handwritten lab notes highlighted. Expect to edit extensively (step order, volumes, lab-specific tweaks, strainer colors, which steps happen at FACS core vs. in-lab, etc.).

Overview

  • Buffer: Miltenyi Nuclei Extraction Buffer (aka lysis buffer; = 4°C, bottom of fridge). For different tissues: 2 mL for 400 mg temporal lobe to increase nuclear density.
  • Equipment: gentleMACS Octo Dissociator; program 4C_nuclei_1 (Miltenyi 4C_nuclei_1 OK to start).
  • Strainers: 70 μm (purple) first filter, 30 μm (yellow) post-resuspension filter. We don't use 100 μm.
  • Resuspension buffer: cold PBS (not the Miltenyi resuspension buffer).
  • Location: performed at the FACS core.

Solutions to be pre-made

Lysis buffer (aka Nuclei Extraction Buffer)

Add RNase inhibitor (final concentration 0.2 U/μL) to chilled 4 mL Nuclei Extraction Buffer per extraction.

Resuspension buffer

Miltenyi nuclei separation buffer. Volume per sample:

  • Up to 50 mg tissue → 1.5 mL
  • 50–100 mg → 3 mL
  • 100–200 mg → 6 mL
  • Mouse brain hemisphere → 8 mL

Ice-cold PBS + 1% BSA

For pre-coating sample tubes, SmartStrainers, and collection tubes.

Take with you to FACS core (handwritten list)

1× Nuclei Extraction Buffer (with RNase inhibitor added)
1× Resuspension buffer (volume per sample size above)
1× Ice-cold PBS + 1% BSA (for pre-coating)
1× Cooler (don't have — may need to order?)
1× gentleMACS C Tube
1 ea 70 μm (purple) + 30 μm (yellow) SmartStrainers
4× 15 mL tubes
1× 1 mL pipette + tips

Reagents & equipment

  • Nuclei Extraction Buffer — Miltenyi 130-128-024 (100 mL / 25 extractions; 2–8°C, do not freeze)
  • MACS SmartStrainers 70 μm (purple) — Miltenyi 130-098-462
  • MACS SmartStrainers 30 μm (yellow) — Miltenyi 130-098-458
  • gentleMACS Octo Dissociator — Miltenyi 130-095-937 (or with Heaters: 130-096-427)
  • gentleMACS C Tubes — Miltenyi 130-093-237 or 130-096-334
  • RNase inhibitor — e.g. murine RNase Inhibitor from NEB
  • (Optional) Eppendorf LoBind 5 mL tubes
  • For nuclei staining (optional): 7-AAD (130-111-568), DAPI (130-111-570), or DRAQ5 (130-117-343)

Procedure

0 of 0 checked

2.1 Sample, equipment, and reagent preparation

#Action
1Pre-cool centrifuge, buffers, and consumables with sample contact (e.g. gentleMACS C Tube and SmartStrainers) at 4°C. Recommended: pre-cool the C Tube containing prepared lysis buffer overnight at 4°C. Ideally perform all nuclei extraction steps in a cold room.
2Preparation of lysis buffer (aka nuclei extraction buffer; = 4°C, bottom of fridge): per extraction, add RNase inhibitor (final conc 0.2 U/μL) to pre-cooled 4 mL Nuclei Extraction Buffer.
3Preparation of resuspension buffer: use nuclei separation buffer (e.g. for Anti-Nucleus MicroBeads 130-132-997). Lab uses cold PBS instead.
Per-extraction volumes recommended:
  • up to 50 mg tissue → 1.5 mL
  • 50–100 mg → 3 mL
  • 100–200 mg → 6 mL
  • Mouse brain hemisphere → 8 mL
4Preparation of tissue sample: resect tissue, cut into pieces of up to 200 mg. Freeze on dry ice or in LN₂. Cut before freezing; if frozen pieces are >200 mg, cut without thawing.

2.2 Nuclei extraction protocol

Work fast until tissue is dissociated; keep samples on ice at all steps. Volumes below are for up to 200 mg tissue per extraction.

Lab adjustments (handwritten, bottom of page):
  1. Reduce volume per 2-2-2.
  2. Mortar + pestle with LOW volume of extraction buffer. Transfer to C Tube. Use MORE extraction buffer to rinse the mortar + pestle.
#Action
1Add 2 mL ice-cold lysis buffer to each pre-cooled gentleMACS C Tube. Don't add all lysis buffer to sample until you get to FACS core.
2Transfer tissue pieces to the gentleMACS C Tube containing lysis buffer and directly proceed with the following steps until samples are dissociated. Do not let frozen sample thaw before dissociation — endogenous RNase might degrade RNA.
3Close gentleMACS C Tube and place it on the gentleMACS Dissociator. Close tightly beyond the first resistance.
4Run gentleMACS Program 4C_nuclei_1 on the gentleMACS Dissociator. Miltenyi 4C_nuclei_1 OK start.
5After termination of the program, detach C Tube from the gentleMACS Dissociator and place the C Tube immediately on ice. Depending on tissue type, additional 5–10 min incubation on ice after dissociation might be needed to optimize cell lysis. Over-lysis causes aggregation/damage.
6Apply nuclei suspension to a MACS SmartStrainer (70 μm, purple; pre-coat with BSA 1:2) placed on a 15 mL tube. For increased nuclei recovery, use low-binding tubes. Go up to Single-Cell Core.
7Wash MACS SmartStrainer with 2 mL ice-cold lysis buffer. Alternatively for max cell recovery, rinse used C Tube with ice-cold lysis buffer before adding to the SmartStrainer.
8Discard MACS SmartStrainer and centrifuge nuclei suspension at 300 × g, 4°C, 5 min. Carefully aspirate supernatant completely. Centrifuge at 10k core.
9Resuspend nuclei pellet with resuspension buffer (volume per sample-size table above) by slowly and gently pipetting the sample up and down ~10 times.
10Apply nuclei suspension to a MACS SmartStrainer (30 μm, yellow; pre-coat with BSA) placed on a 15 mL tube. For myelin-rich brain, a myelin removal step is strongly recommended.
11Collect nuclei suspension and proceed immediately with downstream applications (snRNA-seq or nuclei sorting). If debris amount still too high, purify the suspension using a cell sorter (e.g. MACSQuant Tyto).

2.3 (Optional) Labeling for microscopic or flow cytometric analysis

Lab practice: we use the Sony cell sorter at the FACS core with DAPI provided by the FACS Core (no need to bring your own stain).

#Action
1Add 7-AAD (0.525 μg/mL final) OR DAPI (0.25 μg/mL final) OR DRAQ5 (10 μM final) staining solution to a small fraction of the nuclei suspension. Mix by inverting gently.
2Incubate 5 min at 4°C.
3Load sample on hemocytometer or flow cytometer (e.g. MACSQuant Analyzer; lab uses Sony cell sorter at FACS core).
4Analyze sample according to manufacturer's recommendation.

Key handwritten notes consolidated

  • Buffer vol scaling: 2 mL extraction buffer for 400 mg temporal lobe to increase nuclear density.
  • Lysis buffer stored at 4°C, bottom of fridge.
  • Don't add all lysis buffer to sample until arriving at FACS core.
  • gentleMACS program 4C_nuclei_1 is the right starting program.
  • Strainers used: 70 μm (purple) and 30 μm (yellow). No 100 μm. Pre-coat with BSA (1:2 on the 70 μm, plain BSA on the 30 μm).
  • Centrifuge at the 10k core; "go up to single-cell core" for the 70 μm strainer step.
  • Pre-coat tubes and strainers with ice-cold PBS + 1% BSA.
  • Sony cell sorter at FACS core; DAPI provided by the FACS Core.
  • Lab adjustments summary: (1) reduce volume per 2-2-2; (2) mortar + pestle with LOW volume of extraction buffer, transfer to C Tube, then use more buffer to rinse the mortar + pestle.
  • Cooler may need to be ordered — not currently in lab supply.
